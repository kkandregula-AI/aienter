04 / EVOLUTION

Roles don't disappear. They move up.

Developer in 2035

07 / SCENARIOS

Three futures, side by side

Not a single destiny — a spectrum. Which one an enterprise lands in is a choice, not a certainty.

SCENARIO A

AI-Assisted

Humans stay dominant; AI is a productivity tool.

  • People execute, AI accelerates
  • Headcount stable
  • Lowest risk, slowest gain
SCENARIO B

AI-Augmented

Humans and agents work as one team; AI is a digital workforce.

  • People manage agents
  • New oversight roles emerge
  • Balanced risk and reward
SCENARIO C

AI-Native

AI runs most operational work; humans move to the top of the ladder.

  • Strategy, creativity, judgment
  • Relationships & accountability
  • Highest gain, highest stakes
